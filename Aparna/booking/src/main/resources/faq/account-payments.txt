Frequently Asked Questions — Account & Payments:
Q: Will my real credit card be charged?
A: No, Meridian uses a test/mock payment gateway. No actual financial transactions or charges occur.

Q: How do I update my passport number or contact info?
A: Click on your avatar/name in the top right navbar, select "Profile & Settings", update your information, and click "Save Profile Changes".

Q: How do I log out of my account?
A: Open the user dropdown menu in the top right navbar and click "Sign Out".

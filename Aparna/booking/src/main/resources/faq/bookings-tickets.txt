Frequently Asked Questions — Bookings & Tickets:
Q: How can I cancel a flight booking?
A: Go to "My Bookings", locate your upcoming trip card, click "Cancel Booking", and confirm the cancellation modal.

Q: What happens when I cancel a booking?
A: Your ticket status updates to CANCELLED and your seats are returned to the flight's available seat pool.

Q: Where can I see my ticket reference number?
A: Your ticket reference number is displayed on the Booking Confirmation page and listed on your "My Bookings" page cards.

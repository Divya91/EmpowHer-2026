Frequently Asked Questions — Flights & Search:
Q: How do I filter for nonstop flights?
A: Select the "Nonstop" radio button in the filters panel on the Search Flights page.

Q: Can I change my cabin class after searching?
A: Yes, you can choose between Economy, Premium Economy, and Business Class during seat selection.

Q: What baggage is included?
A: Each standard ticket includes one carry-on bag and one checked bag up to 23kg.

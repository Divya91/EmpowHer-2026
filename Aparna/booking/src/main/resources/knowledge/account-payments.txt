Meridian Account & Payment Policy Reference Knowledge:
- User Authentication: Account registration (`POST /api/users/signup`) and Login (`POST /api/users/login`) store user profile data under localStorage key `meridian.auth.user`.
- Profile Management: Registered users can manage Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Passport Details, and Travel Preferences under "Profile & Settings".
- Payment System: Meridian operates on a simulated Mock Payment flow for testing and educational purposes. No real credit card or payment gateway processing occurs.
- Payment inputs require Name on Card, 16-digit Card Number, Expiry (MM/YY), and CVV for client-side form validation before ticket confirmation.
